# CORE - Brand Mention Tracking Platform

## What is CORE?

CORE is a platform that helps businesses understand how their brand is mentioned across multiple AI language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and others. Think of it as a radar system that scans what these AI assistants say about your brand when people ask questions online.

## What Does It Do?

### Track Your Brand Mentions
CORE monitors how your brand appears in responses from various AI models. When someone asks an AI assistant a question related to your industry, CORE checks whether your brand is mentioned, how positively it's discussed, and where it ranks compared to your competitors.

### Geographic Intelligence (GEO)
The platform includes geographic features that allow you to track brand mentions by location. You can see how your brand is perceived in different countries and languages, helping you understand your global presence across AI platforms.

### Competitive Analysis
CORE doesn't just track your brand—it also monitors your competitors. You can see who else is being mentioned in AI responses, how often they appear, and where opportunities exist to improve your own visibility.

### Automated Monitoring
Set up automated scans that run on a schedule (daily, weekly, or custom intervals) to continuously track how your brand performs across different AI models. Get alerts when your visibility changes or when competitors gain ground.

## How It Works

1. **Set Up Your Brand**: Enter your brand name, industry, and geographic focus
2. **Define Your Questions**: Create a list of questions that potential customers might ask AI assistants about your industry
3. **Choose AI Models**: Select which AI platforms you want to monitor (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, etc.)
4. **Run Analysis**: CORE queries all selected AI models with your questions and analyzes the responses
5. **Review Results**: See detailed reports showing where your brand appears, sentiment analysis, competitor comparisons, and opportunities for improvement
